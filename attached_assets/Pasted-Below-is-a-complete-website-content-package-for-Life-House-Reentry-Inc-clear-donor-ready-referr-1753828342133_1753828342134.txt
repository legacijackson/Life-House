2) ABOUT
Headline: About Life House Reentry Inc.
Lead: We provide safe housing and holistic support for formerly incarcerated people, helping them reenter society with dignity, purpose, and a path to financial independence.

Mission (plain-language)
We provide safe, stable housing and personalized life-skills support to help formerly incarcerated individuals successfully reenter society with dignity, purpose, and a path to financial independence.

Vision
We aim to break the cycle of incarceration by creating a network of Life Houses where every returning citizen has access to healing, housing, and the tools to build a better life.

Our Approach (7-Stage Resident Transformation)

Intake – Stabilization, orientation, paperwork, benefits, ID, immediate needs.

Design – Life design, values, goals, spiritual grounding.

Training – Financial literacy, credit repair, credentials, accelerated programs.

Working – Sustainable income; resident contributes up to 30% (25% saved, 5% brokerage).

Overflow – Income grows; above-cap contributions support program; peer mentoring starts.

Transition – Permanent rental or first-home purchase with savings returned.

Legacy – Alumni coaching, mentorship, community leadership.

3) PROGRAMS
Overview
Our programs meet people where they are and move them toward income, savings, and permanent housing.

Transitional Housing
Safe, structured homes with clear expectations, curfews, chore systems, visitor policy, and supportive culture. Two per room, shared bath; monthly housekeeping; landscaping and safety checks.

Life Design & Coaching
Weekly workshops + biweekly coaching focused on identity, goals, planning, and accountability. Residents learn to design the life they want—and build it step by step.

Financial Literacy & Wealth Basics
Budgeting, banking, credit repair, debt strategies, saving and investing fundamentals. Our model channels 25% of income into savings (reimbursable at exit) and 5% into a resident-owned brokerage.

Career Preparation & Placement
Resume, interviews, job search, employer partnerships, trades and accelerated training pathways. We target living-wage outcomes and stack credentials to increase earnings.

Reentry & Benefits Navigation
ID restoration, Medi‑Cal/CalAIM, CalFresh, GA/SSI referrals, ECM care coordination, STOP alignment, legal aid referrals, child support navigation, transportation planning.

Family & Community Reintegration
Family visits, reunification support, parenting classes, monthly family meals, community service, alumni mentorship.

Program Alignment (for funders/referrers)

STOP (CDCR): 0–180 days + extension eligibility; daily attendance, service notes, reporting.

CalAIM ECM & Community Supports: Care coordination; housing transition/tenancy supports; managed care plan billing & documentation.

Local Workforce & Education: American Job Centers, adult schools, trade programs, apprenticeships.

4) RESIDENTS — APPLY
Headline: Apply for Housing & Support
Intro: If you’re returning home and ready to work on goals, we’re ready to help. Our homes are safe, structured, and focused on your success.

Who Can Apply

Adults returning from incarceration (Sacramento region)

Able to live in a shared environment and follow house rules

Willing to engage in weekly workshops, coaching, and job/education activities

Referred by self, family, parole/probation, STOP, ECM/care manager, or community partner

Program Contribution
Income-based. Residents contribute up to 30% of monthly income: 25% saved (returned at exit) + 5% to a resident-owned brokerage. Many costs may be covered or offset by STOP/CalAIM/partners.

What to Expect

Beds available for 4–6 residents per home (two per room).

90-day initial term with extensions based on progress (up to 24 months).

Weekly workshops + coaching; clear rules and support.

9) FAQ
Q: Who can live at Life House?
Adults returning from incarceration who can live cooperatively and commit to programming.

Q: How long can residents stay?
Initial 90 days with extensions based on progress, up to 24 months when appropriate.

Q: How much does it cost?
Income-based. Residents contribute up to 30% of income (25% saved, 5% brokerage). STOP/CalAIM/partners may offset costs.

Q: Do you offer counseling?
We offer non‑clinical coaching and life design. We connect residents to licensed clinical care as needed.

Q: Can parole or ECM refer?
Yes. We coordinate with parole/probation, STOP, and managed care plans.

Q: Do you accept donations or in‑kind?
